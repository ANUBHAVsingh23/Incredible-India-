Welcome to India

Discover the country and its remarkable diversity.

India, officially the Republic of India, is a country in South Asia. It is known for its ancient civilizations, diverse languages, religions, cuisines, festivals, art forms and architectural heritage. From the Himalayan mountains in the north to tropical coastlines in the south, India's geography and cultural traditions create an extraordinary variety of experiences.
